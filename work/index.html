Work

Android, iOS and the backend behind them, mostly in payments.

Roles

  1. Oct 2022 – Oct 2026

    Paythru, London

    Senior Android Developer from March 2024, Android Developer before that

    Paythru makes payments software for EV charging. In a team of ten, I shipped charging and payment features across about six white-label apps, mostly in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with some SwiftUI on iOS. I integrated Adyen for in-app and terminal payments and reworked the Bitrise pipeline so builds could go from commit to the stores, which cut release time from three or four days to under 24 hours.

    In 2026 I built Paythru's new TypeScript payments platform on AWS, including the infrastructure, with idempotent payment handling, an audit log, monitoring and alarms.
